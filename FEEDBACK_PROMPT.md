# Student feedback prompt — ready for opt-in, asynchronous testing

The pilot is a free, browser-local **learning aid**, not an official SPSS/jamovi alternative. If you are currently conducting an MA/MSc psychology project, try it using the **synthetic dataset** or an anonymized CSV and answer:

1. What is the actual research question you needed to answer? (Do not share personal participant data.)
2. Did the tool recommend or support the statistical test you needed?
3. Did the APA explanation help you understand how to defend the result?
4. What did you have to open jamovi/SPSS/ChatGPT to finish?
5. Would a reliable tool that solved that remaining need be worth ₹199 to you now? Why or why not?
6. May we quote your anonymized feedback publicly? (Explicit opt-in only.)

Do not record identifiable participant datasets, research subjects' private information, or survey responses without separate informed permission.
