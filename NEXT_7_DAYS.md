# Seven-day build → distribute → earn → adapt loop

Date: 22 October 2026

### Day 1: Working narrow pilot (completed in local environment)

Only two tests: Pearson correlation and independent-samples Welch t-test. CSV import, plain-language results, APA paragraph, basic assumptions and viva preparation. No paid AI backend. Statistical unit tests and SciPy comparison pass. Browser QA outstanding due local browser policy.

### Day 2: Public release gate

Create a dedicated GitHub repository; deploy the static source to an isolated free site. Check the result in desktop Chrome and Android Chrome. Test two externally sourced, anonymized datasets against jamovi or SciPy. Implement optional privacy-safe event counting and a real opt-in feedback form before counting users. Do not send participant CSV content to analytics.

### Days 3–4: Distribution

Publish two useful lessons rather than generic ads: “Pearson vs Spearman for MA Psychology: which one is defensible?” and “How to explain a t-test during your viva.” Give a clear, useful sample and label the tool as a free pilot. Share in relevant student groups only with community permission; publish public documentation that can be indexed; offer it to student creators teaching statistics. No mass DMs or cold sales calls.

### Day 5: Observe usage and friction

Check unique opt-in users, started analyses, completed analyses, errors and feedback. Do not claim conversion rates from sample-clicks by the founder. Interview-style feedback can be collected asynchronously without calls. Collect the one unsupported analysis students wanted most.

### Day 6: Commercial signal

Show an explicit price proposition of ₹199 per study report / analysis. If the product has earned trust and billing is authorized and configured, run a true checkout experiment. Otherwise log paid intent separately as weaker evidence. Do not pretend an email waitlist or button click is revenue.

### Day 7: Decision gate

- **Continue:** At least 20 genuine students tried, at least 5 completed their own anonymized data analysis, and there is tangible paid demand or unusually strong repeat need.
- **Iterate:** Meaningful users but weak payments → test alternative offer (e.g. dissertation methods coach, exam/viva pack) and price.
- **Stop/reposition:** Generic workflow easily reproduced with jamovi + ChatGPT and no meaningful evidence of demand.

Proposed numeric thresholds are screening heuristics, not guaranteed statistically valid business validation.

## What has not yet happened

No students have used this build, no independent payment attempt has occurred, no public deployment exists, no analytics instrumentation is connected. Revenue: ₹0.
