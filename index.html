IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

Your judgment
still matters.

This is a learning pilot, not a replacement for a research supervisor or a validated statistical suite. It supports Pearson correlation and independent-samples Welch t-tests only. It does not perform Shapiro–Wilk tests, reliability analysis, paired comparisons, regression, clinical inference, or power analysis.

It cannot verify sampling quality, measurement validity, independence, causal design or ethical approval. Skewness observations are descriptive screens, not formal normality tests. Data and calculations stay on this device; the hosting platform may log routine website requests.

Do not upload identifiable participant data. Do not invent or modify observations to achieve a significant result.
